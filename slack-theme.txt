Harvest theme for Slack

Slack keeps its theme in your account, so apply it by hand:
1. Slack > Preferences (Cmd+,) > Appearance or Themes.
2. Set Slack to Dark mode, then choose the custom theme option.
3. Enter these colors:

   System navigation    #1C140F
   Selected items       #E8863A
   Presence indication  #5FB3C4
   Notifications        #E9B949

If Slack offers a single box for pasting a theme, paste this line:
#1C140F,#E8863A,#5FB3C4,#E9B949

To turn it off, choose one of Slack's built-in themes again.
